  THE SIMILARITY HALF MISSED 4 OF 4 REWORDED REPEATS. Once the deciding
  fields are taken out, what is left is two or three words — "vegetarian
  meal" against "with veg meal" — and the encoder scored the weakest of these
  pairs 0.5450, well under the shipped band. The instrument that was introduced
  to recognise rewording is now being asked about fragments too short to carry
  much meaning, and on these it does not recognise it.

  AND THE BASELINE'S FAILURE IS STILL PRESENT, MOVED INSIDE THE FREE TEXT. The
  worst true difference here scored 0.9346 and would be suppressed: one word of
  negation changes which ticket is bought, and the encoder reads the two
  phrasings as the same topic — exactly the mistake the deciding-field gate
  was built to remove. The gate removed it only for the fields it knows.

  So the gate's gain on the corpus above is not a gain for similarity. It is
  what happens when the decision is taken away from similarity. Where the
  decision is left to it, these eight pairs say it is still the wrong
  instrument. Eight constructed pairs are not evidence of a rate; they are
  evidence that the failure mode survives the fix.

WHAT THE HYBRID ARMS ARE, AND WHAT THEY ARE NOT

 THE HYBRID COMPARATOR IS OFF BY DEFAULT. The engine still ships the
 pure-semantic path measured in docs/results/semantic-experiment-minilm.txt,
 and the semantic rows in this run reproduce that file. Nothing here
 withdraws it: this is the next step, measured.

 WHAT IS REAL in the hybrid rows:
   - the verdicts come from the engine's own semantic.Checker with its field
     extractor configured, not from a reimplementation in this harness;
   - the gate's decisions are exact string comparisons of extracted values,
     so they are reproducible from the journaled intent text alone;
   - the latency figures are real timings of that path.

 WHAT IS NOT:
   - the labels are still generated by construction, not hand-assigned, so
     every accuracy figure here remains provisional in exactly the way the
     baseline's does;
   - the field extractor's vocabulary was written against this corpus, so its
     coverage of it is not evidence of coverage anywhere else. A phrasing
     outside that vocabulary is not read at all;
   - no embedding was needed for any pair in the corpus, so these rows do not
     measure semantic similarity. They measure exact matching over scraped
     fields. The free-text probe above is the only part of this run in which
     the similarity half of the comparator decided anything.

 The honest reading: the gate removes the baseline's false positives because
 it stops asking a question similarity cannot answer. It does not make
 similarity better at that question — the free-text probe above shows the same
 failure surviving inside the text the gate does not cover — and it needs a
 per-activity schema the engine cannot derive on its own. The durable fix is
 for an activity to DECLARE its deciding fields in its input, so that nothing
 has to be scraped and nothing that decides identity is left to a threshold.
