WHAT THESE NUMBERS ARE, AND WHAT THEY ARE NOT

 REAL MEASUREMENTS in this run:
   - the per-check latency figures (p50/p99): real wall-clock timings of the
     engine's own exact.Key and semantic.Checker code paths, with the
     embeddings served from the local cache;
   - one real model round-trip, measured on the 68 call(s) the cache could not
     serve: p50 32.65ms, p99 114.16ms;
   - the exact-match arm's accuracy: byte comparison has no model in it, so
     its numbers are exactly what the baseline would score on this corpus;
   - the behaviour of the thresholds and the escalation band.

 NOT YET MEASURED, and awaiting work no program can do:
   - the ground-truth labels were generated by construction (no human labelled these pairs).
     Until a person labels real retry pairs, every accuracy figure here is
     provisional. A published result needs a hand-labelled set and a stated
     inter-annotator agreement; this harness measures the MECHANISM, not the
     claim the mechanism is supposed to support.

 Do not put the semantic accuracy numbers from this run in the report as
 results. Put the latency numbers in, and put the accuracy numbers in only
 once this harness has been re-run with a real encoder over a hand-labelled
 corpus.
